# Design Brief

## Style: Earthly Delight

The design should evoke a sense of natural wonder, with earthy tones and organic shapes that resemble the wilderness. The layout should be intuitive and easy to navigate, with ample use of white space to create a sense of breathability. The overall atmosphere should be inviting and community-driven, with a focus on showcasing the beauty of wild foods and the people who forage them.

## Design Tokens

| Aspect | Direction |
|--------|-----------|
| Colors | Muted greens and browns, with pops of vibrant colors to represent the diversity of wild foods |
| Typography | Friendly, approachable sans-serif fonts with a natural, handwritten feel |
| Components |  Buttons and cards should have a natural, earthy feel, with subtle texture and rounded corners |
| Imagery | High-quality images of wild foods, foragers in action, and lush landscapes |

## Theme Settings

- **Theme Color:** green
- **Mode:** light
- **Border Radius:** 0.5

## App Variant: ForagePro

> Wild food at your doorstep

ForagePro is a premium subscription-based platform that connects experienced foragers with local businesses and restaurants, providing a reliable supply chain for wild ingredients. It focuses on building a network of trusted suppliers and buyers, ensuring high-quality products and promoting sustainable foraging practices. By targeting the B2B market, ForagePro aims to establish itself as a go-to platform for the culinary industry.

**Target Audience:** Professional chefs, restaurants, and food establishments
**Monetization:** Subscription fees for suppliers and buyers, commission on sales
**Complexity:** premium
